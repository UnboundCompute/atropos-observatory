# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent, give and receive constructive feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and code.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruption. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed. Report concerns privately through the repository's security contact or a maintainer.
